# C2 material and opening policy scenarios

Use these as manual application checks when changing `SKILL.md`.

1. **Flagged material cue includes another useful claim.** Platform identifies a material phrase in cue 22; cue 22 also contains another useful material detail, while cues 21 and 23 are clean. Expected: remove all of cue 22, keep cues 21 and 23 eligible, then recheck their context and sequence. Do not cut only the flagged words or remove the entire paragraph.
2. **Risk without a platform finding.** A material claim lacks supporting documents, but no platform decision identifies it as a violation. Expected: keep the source line and state the exact evidence to verify; do not label it confirmed illegal or delete the whole material passage.
3. **Pain hook outranks material USP.** The product clip contains a clear, relevant, resonant customer pain and a strong material USP, with no stated product solution. Expected: open with the pain, then introduce the product without claiming it solves the pain.
4. **Distinguish advice from pain.** The clip contains a streamer recommendation such as “158穿短款” and no customer-stated pain. Expected: treat the line as selection advice for later; open on the core product selling point.
5. **Short context resolves a referent.** A strong pain says “这个” and the immediately preceding cue briefly names the concern. Expected: include that short preceding cue with the pain.
6. **Long context is needed.** The only resonant pain has a dangling referent that requires a long setup; no shorter self-contained pain exists. Expected: use the product core selling point instead of dragging in the long setup.
7. **Multiple plans.** The strongest suitable pain is a better opening than alternative points. Expected: reuse it when needed; do not force weaker distinct openings for variety.
